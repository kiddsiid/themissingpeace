01 · What it is

A creative playground that quietly produces a practical, trustworthy plan

The Missing Peace gathers what a couple wants the day to feel like, turns those wishes into a durable Wedding Compass, and carries that Compass through every decision — meal, money, guests, vendors, timeline, seating, documents, and the guest experience. It should feel intimate before it feels technical, visual before it feels administrative, and trustworthy before it feels magical. Poetry invites the decision; the interface makes the consequence clear.

05 · The Wedding Compass

The product contract

Dream does not just produce a sentence and floating clouds. It produces a structured Compass every module can read without asking the couple to repeat themselves. It keeps both the poetic phrase and the practical interpretation — “one long, generous table” stays human, but the engine also understands it implies family-style service, communal presentation, higher coordination, and household-proximate seating.

06 · The Peace Engine

From a single score to honest readiness

The engine answers three questions continuously: what is the couple trying to protect, what is at risk, and what is the most helpful next step. It combines deterministic rules with optional generated guidance — which may draft or explain, but must never silently confirm a fact. It produces four projections: Next action, Watch list, Ripple map, and Readiness.

08 · Ripple behavior

One change, visibly traveling

The product feels like one engine only when a decision moves through the world. A ripple has a source, affected objects, a reason, and the user's choice to accept, review, or dismiss. A quiet “This will ripple into…” preview appears before any major change.

09 · Visual system

Soft, editorial, and alive

Cormorant Garamond for display and emotional moments; Manrope for interface; JetBrains Mono for figures. Italic serif carries intentions and stories, never essential instructions. The central surface feels alive because it changes — not because it is covered in animation.

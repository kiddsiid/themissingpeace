The fine print, kindly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026 · Applies to the early-access preview at themissingpeace.pages.dev

Plain-language summary: we collect only what you type into this site, we use it to send you your Wedding Compass and early-access updates, we never sell it, and one email to us deletes it.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your first name, email address, and — only if you choose — your partner's email, wedding date, planning stage, and the Compass the Dream Walk wrote for you.
  • Payments: if you buy a founding seat, payment is processed by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe, never to us; we receive only confirmation of the payment, the offer purchased, and the email you used.
  • Usage: simple first-party analytics events (for example, "Dream Walk completed"), a random browser identifier, and any campaign tags (UTM/referral) in the link that brought you here. No third-party ad trackers.
  • In your browser: your Dream Walk answers live in your own browser's local storage until you choose to save them with us.

How we use it

  • To email you your Wedding Compass and essential messages about your saved Compass, reservation, or purchase (this is the delivery consent you tick when saving).
  • To send occasional inspiration and product news only if you separately opt in — unsubscribe anytime with one click.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't run third-party advertising trackers. We don't email your partner unless you gave us their address for that purpose.

Where it lives

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, and payment records with Stripe. Both process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your choices

Email us at hello@themissingpeace.app to see, correct, export, or delete anything we hold about you. Deletion requests are honored in full, including analytics rows tied to your saved record.

Early-access caveat

The Missing Peace is an early-access product. As the product grows this policy will grow with it; material changes will be announced by email to everyone with a saved Compass before they take effect.

      Fair questions

      Is the Dream Walk really free?

      Yes — the Dream Walk and your Wedding Compass are free, with no account and no card. Saving your Compass takes just a first name and an email so we can send it to you.

      What exactly is "early access"?

      The Missing Peace is a young product being built in the open. Parts of what this page shows are working today in our prototype (you can wander a demo wedding); the rest ships to founding members wave by wave. We label previews as previews — if a screen is a prototype, we say so.

      What happens after I pay for a founding seat?

      Stripe confirms your payment and emails a receipt; we hold your seat and email your invitation when your access wave opens. Cancel before your access begins for a full refund, and there's a 14-day full refund after access begins — one email is enough.

      What do you do with my email?

      We send your Compass and the essential messages about your saved plan and early access. Anything more — inspiration, product news — only if you tick the separate optional box, and you can unsubscribe anytime. Details in the privacy policy.

      I'm a wedding planner — is this for me?

      Yes. A Founding Planner seat covers a multi-wedding workspace, so every client couple gets the same engine. Professional access follows the first couples wave.
